# Cryptocurrency, Web3 & Blockchain Glossary

This glossary standardizes terms in cryptocurrency, Web3 protocols, decentralized finance (DeFi), and distributed consensus mechanisms in Georgian text.

### Crypto Terminology Guidelines

1. **Coin & Token Orthography**:
   - Digital currencies retain Georgian nominative vowel `-ი` when transliterated: `ბიტკოინი`, `ეთერიუმი` (not `ბიტკოინ`, `ეთერიუმ`).
   - Standard acronyms stay in Latin: `BTC`, `ETH`, `SOL`, `USDT`, `USDC`.
2. **Decentralized Compounds**:
   - Single-word compounds: `კრიპტოვალუტა`, `კრიპტობირჟა`, `კრიპტოაქტივი`.
   - Two-word phrases: `ჭკვიანი კონტრაქტი`, `ცივი საფულე`, `ცხელი საფულე`, `კერძო გასაღები`, `საჯარო გასაღები`.
3. **Avoid Clumsy Calques**:
   - `fork` is transliterated in technical blockchain prose as `ფორკი` (მყარი ფორკი / რბილი ფორკი), not translated as the kitchen utensil `ჩანგალი`.
   - `whitepaper` is `თეთრი წიგნი` (or in Latin `whitepaper`).
   - `airdrop` is `ერდროპი` (not `აერდროპი`).

## Standard Terminology Table

| Latin / English | Preferred Georgian | Avoid (Incorrect / Russianism) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| blockchain | **ბლოკჩეინი** | — | Distributed, decentralized cryptographic ledger. |
| cryptocurrency | **კრიპტოვალუტა** | `კრიპტო ვალუტა` | Digital currency secured by cryptography. Written as one word. |
| Bitcoin (BTC) | **ბიტკოინი** | `ბიტკოინ` | Spelled with final nominative -ი: ბიტკოინი. |
| Ethereum (ETH) | **ეთერიუმი** | `ეთერიუმ` | Spelled with final nominative -ი: ეთერიუმი. |
| mining | **მაინინგი** | — | Validating transactions and minting new tokens via compute work. |
| miner | **მაინერი** | — | Node or hardware operator participating in proof-of-work consensus. |
| crypto wallet | **კრიპტოგრაფიული საფულე** | — | Application or hardware device managing cryptographic keys. |
| cold wallet | **ცივი საფულე** | — | Offline cryptocurrency storage disconnected from the internet. |
| hot wallet | **ცხელი საფულე** | — | Cryptocurrency wallet connected to the internet. |
| hardware wallet | **აპარატურული საფულე** | — | Physical device (e.g. Ledger, Trezor) storing private keys in a secure enclave. |
| smart contract | **ჭკვიანი კონტრაქტი** | — | Self-executing digital agreement deployed on a blockchain. |
| decentralized finance (DeFi) | **დეცენტრალიზებული ფინანსები** | — | Financial services built on smart contracts without traditional intermediaries. |
| decentralized application (dApp) | **დეცენტრალიზებული აპლიკაცია** | — | Application running on a distributed network. |
| gas fee | **გაზის საკომისიო** | — | Execution fee paid to network validators for processing transactions. |
| consensus mechanism | **კონსენსუსის მექანიზმი** | — | Fault-tolerant protocol ensuring all nodes agree on ledger state. |
| Proof of Work (PoW) | **სამუშაოს მტკიცებულება** | — | Consensus model requiring computational resource expenditure. |
| Proof of Stake (PoS) | **წილის მტკიცებულება** | — | Consensus model requiring validators to lock up financial tokens. |
| hash rate | **ჰეშრეიტი** | — | Aggregate computational speed of all miners on a PoW network. |
| halving | **ჰალვინგი** | — | Programmatic 50% reduction in miner block rewards every 210,000 blocks. |
| non-fungible token (NFT) | **არაურთიერთშემცვლელი ტოკენი** | — | Unique digital certificate of ownership recorded on a blockchain. |
| stablecoin | **სტეიბლკოინი** | — | Cryptocurrency pegged to a stable asset (e.g. USD, EUR, gold). |
| altcoin | **ალტკოინი** | — | Any alternative cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin. |
| memecoin | **მემკოინი** | — | Cryptocurrency originating from an internet meme or viral community. |
| staking | **სტეიკინგი** | — | Locking tokens in a PoS network to support validation and earn yield. |
| validator | **ვალიდატორი** | — | Participant in a PoS network responsible for verifying new blocks. |
| node | **კვანძი** | — | Individual computer connected to a blockchain network. |
| full node | **სრული კვანძი** | — | Node storing the complete historical ledger of the blockchain. |
| airdrop | **ერდროპი** | `აერდროპი` | Free distribution of crypto tokens to community wallet addresses. |
| whitepaper | **თეთრი წიგნი** | `ვაითპეიპერი` | Technical manifesto outlining the architecture and goals of a crypto project. |
| private key | **კერძო გასაღები** | `პრივატული გასაღები` | Secret cryptographic alphanumeric string granting spending authority. |
| public key | **საჯარო გასაღები** | — | Cryptographic key publicly shared to generate wallet addresses. |
| seed phrase / recovery phrase | **აღდგენის ფრაზა** | — | Ordered sequence of 12 or 24 mnemonic words backing up private keys. |
| crypto exchange | **კრიპტობირჟა** | `კრიპტო ბირჟა` | Platform for trading digital assets. Written as one word. |
| centralized exchange (CEX) | **ცენტრალიზებული ბირჟა** | — | Custodial trading venue operated by an enterprise (e.g. Binance, Coinbase). |
| decentralized exchange (DEX) | **დეცენტრალიზებული ბირჟა** | — | Peer-to-peer non-custodial trading venue operated by smart contracts (e.g. Uniswap). |
| liquidity pool | **ლიკვიდურობის აუზი** | — | Crowdsourced pool of tokens locked in a smart contract facilitating trades. |
| liquidity provider (LP) | **ლიკვიდურობის მიმწოდებელი** | — | User who deposits token pairs into liquidity pools to earn fees. |
| automated market maker (AMM) | **ავტომატური მარკეტ-მეიკერი** | — | Algorithmic pricing mechanism governing DEX liquidity pools. |
| yield farming | **მოსავლიანობის ფერმერობა** | — | Lending or staking crypto assets in DeFi protocols to maximize returns. |
| Layer 1 (L1) | **პირველი დონის ქსელი** | — | Base foundational blockchain protocol (e.g. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana). |
| Layer 2 (L2) | **მეორე დონის ქსელი** | — | Secondary framework or scaling protocol built on top of Layer 1. |
| rollup | **როლაპი** | — | L2 scaling solution batching and executing transactions off-chain. |
| optimistic rollup | **ოპტიმისტური როლაპი** | — | Rollup assuming transactions are valid unless challenged via fraud proofs. |
| zero-knowledge rollup (ZK-rollup) | **ნულოვანი ცოდნის როლაპი** | — | Rollup using succinct cryptographic validity proofs (SNARKs/STARKs). |
| zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) | **ნულოვანი ცოდნის მტკიცებულება** | — | Cryptographic method proving statement validity without revealing underlying data. |
| blockchain oracle | **ორაკული** | — | Third-party service feeding external off-chain data into smart contracts. |
| fork | **ფორკი** | `ჩანგალი` | Split in the software rules of a blockchain network. 'ჩანგალი' is a clumsy literal calque. |
| hard fork | **მყარი ფორკი** | — | Backward-incompatible protocol update splitting the blockchain. |
| soft fork | **რბილი ფორკი** | — | Backward-compatible protocol update. |
| tokenomics | **ტოკენომიკა** | — | Economic model, issuance schedule, and utility incentives of a crypto token. |
| Initial Coin Offering (ICO) | **პირველადი მონეტარული შეთავაზება** | — | Fundraising mechanism selling newly minted digital tokens to early backers. |
| Initial DEX Offering (IDO) | **პირველადი DEX-შეთავაზება** | — | Token launch executed on a decentralized liquidity exchange. |
| DAO | **დეცენტრალიზებული ავტონომიური ორგანიზაცია** | — | Community-governed organization managed by rules encoded in smart contracts. |
| governance token | **მმართველობითი ტოკენი** | — | Token granting voting rights on protocol parameters and treasury spend. |
| cross-chain bridge | **ჯვარედინი ხიდი** | — | Protocol enabling value and data transfer between independent blockchains. |
| interoperability | **ურთიერთთავსებადობა** | — | Ability of disparate blockchain ecosystems to communicate and exchange data. |
| immutability | **შეუქცევადობა** | — | Ledger attribute ensuring finalized transactions cannot be modified or erased. |
| impermanent loss | **დროებითი დანაკარგი** | — | Paper loss experienced by LP providers due to divergent volatility of pooled tokens. |
| slippage | **ფასის ცურვა** | — | Difference between expected execution price of a trade and final fulfilled price. |
| gas limit | **გაზის ლიმიტი** | — | Maximum units of gas an account authorizer is willing to spend on a transaction. |
| satoshi | **სატოში** | — | Smallest unit of Bitcoin (0.00000001 BTC). |
| gwei | **გვეი** | — | Denomination of ether used for gas calculations (10^-9 ETH). |
| Total Value Locked (TVL) | **დაბლოკილი მთლიანი ღირებულება** | — | Aggregate dollar value of crypto assets committed into DeFi protocols. |
| rug pull | **რაგ-პული** | — | Malicious exit scam where developers abandon project and steal investors' funds. |
| fiat currency | **ფიატური ვალუტა** | — | Government-issued legal tender not backed by physical commodities (e.g. USD, EUR, GEL). |
| crypto asset | **კრიპტოაქტივი** | — | Broad regulatory classification encompassing digital currencies and tokens. |
| self-custodial wallet | **თვითმეურვეობითი საფულე** | — | Wallet where user maintains exclusive control over private keys. |
| custodial wallet | **მეურვეობითი საფულე** | — | Wallet where a third party holds and safeguards private keys. |
