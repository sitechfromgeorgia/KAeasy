## Words that are English in disguise

Do not insert English words when a Georgian word exists: ❌ `save ფაილები`, `userland წვდომა`,
`tethered ჰაკი`. Use the Georgian word, explain it once, or keep only a real product name (Latin, hyphenated
ending).

`AI-ები` (plural of AI) → `AI მოდელები`, `AI ხელსაწყოები`, `აგენტები`.
